Nano Cortex Controller

in the browser
Not connected

Connect

Chrome or Edge on a computer or Android phone. Click CONNECT and choose your Nano Cortex. The Nano takes one connection at a time: close the editor and switch off a controller board first.

Use it

The screen works like the touch screen: tap, hold, swipe. The eight footswitches below work like the real ones: click, or hold for 0.6 s for their held function. Keys 1–8 press them too.

MIDI

The MIDI button on the screen uses the MIDI inputs of this computer, for example an MC6 over USB. Banks, symbols and FX presets are stored in this browser.
